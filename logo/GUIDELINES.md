# bit logo

Mark: two pixel chevrons `>>`, the chain operator. Built on a square cell grid, 7 cells tall.

## Files
- `bit-symbol.svg`: the mark. Also used as the small-size cut (`bit-symbol-small.svg`), it holds at 16 px.
- `bit-lockup.svg`: mark plus the word `bit`. Black and white versions: `bit-lockup-black.svg`, `bit-lockup-white.svg`.
- `bit-symbol-black.svg`, `bit-symbol-white.svg`: one-colour marks.
- `favicon.ico`, `favicon.svg`, `apple-touch-icon.png`, `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`, `maskable-512.png`: web icons. Paste `head-snippet.html` into the page head.
- `old-gear/`: the previous gear-and-server mark, kept for reference.

## Colour
Black and white only. Black on white or white on black. No greys, no gradients.

## Clear space
Keep one cell (1/7 of the mark height) free on every side, two cells next to the word.

## Minimum size
16 px tall.

## Do not
Stretch, rotate, outline, add shadows, recolour, or smooth the pixel steps.
